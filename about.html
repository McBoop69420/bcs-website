What We Do

We help organizations move from findings to decisions to action to improvement — not just another report.

At Bluegrass Cybersecurity Solutions, we help small and midsize organizations identify real threats, close real gaps, and build security programs that hold up under pressure — not just on the day of the assessment, but months later when it matters.

We take a hands-on approach — assessing your actual risk exposure, strengthening your controls, and making sure the people, vendors, and AI tools around you aren’t your weakest link. Where compliance is required, we make sure your program satisfies it without letting it become the whole story.

We’re independent of any MSP, software vendor, or AI platform, so every recommendation is built around your risk instead of someone else’s product. And we’re based in Kentucky, working with the small and midsize organizations that don’t have an in-house security team of their own.

Bluegrass Cybersecurity Solutions closes the gap between findings and fixes — turning what an assessment finds into what your team actually does about it.

Jared Luyster – Principal Consultant

I help organizations close the gap between security findings and real-world fixes. With a background in IT management and cybersecurity, I have worked directly with organizations to assess their risk exposure, strengthen their controls, and build security programs that function under real-world conditions — not just on paper.

What sets my approach apart is practicality. I do not hand over generic templates and disappear. I work alongside your team to implement controls, document decisions, and make sure your security posture is genuinely defensible. As AI changes how businesses operate, I help clients figure out which new risks actually apply to them — instead of reacting to every headline. I also provide independent oversight of managed service providers, helping organizations understand what their MSP manages, what they still own, and whether the services in place actually support their risk and governance requirements.

Security should not feel overwhelming. It should feel structured, manageable, and built to last. That is what I help you build.
