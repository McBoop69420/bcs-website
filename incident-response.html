Incident response support helps teams define severity levels, escalation paths, decision authority, notification considerations, evidence handling, tabletop scenarios, and post-incident remediation. The focus is a practical plan that can be followed during real disruption.

What this helps with

  • Incident response plan creation or refresh
  • Tabletop exercise preparation
  • Communications and escalation structure
  • Post-incident lessons learned tracking

Typical Deliverables

  • Incident response plan
  • Escalation and contact matrix
  • Tabletop exercise guide
  • After-action tracker
