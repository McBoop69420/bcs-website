IT Exam Prep

Get your policies, risk work, vendor files, and remediation evidence organized before the request list arrives.

Talk about exam prep All services

Reduce scramble time and repeat-finding risk.

Exam prep support builds exam readiness around the practical evidence examiners and auditors usually ask for: policy approvals, risk assessments, BCP testing, vendor due diligence, incident response documentation, access reviews, and remediation status. The goal is a cleaner package and a calmer process.

What this helps with

  • Pre-exam evidence organization
  • Prior exam finding review
  • Management and board packet preparation
  • Gap triage before the exam window
