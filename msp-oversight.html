MSP Oversight

Get independent clarity on what your MSP manages, what you own, and where security accountability really sits.

Talk about MSP oversight All services

Keep control of your environment even when technology is outsourced.

MSP oversight is a vendor-neutral review that helps leadership understand service scope, privileged access, tool coverage, contract terms, reporting gaps, and security responsibilities. It is especially useful before renewals, after incidents, or when examiner questions expose unclear ownership.

What this helps with

  • MSP contract and SLA review
  • Admin access and control validation
  • Security service coverage checks
  • Renewal preparation and spend clarity
