Governance, Risk, and Compliance — Built for How SMBs Actually Operate

AI is the newest risk. It won’t be the last. GRC is how you manage whatever comes next without starting over each time.

Start With Risk, Not Technology

Every organization uses technology differently. GRC starts by understanding yours.

Instead of recommending a product on day one, a GRC approach starts by understanding how your business operates, how technology (including AI) is used, what information is involved, and which obligations already apply.
