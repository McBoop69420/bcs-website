A process you can repeat

A structured, repeatable way to manage cybersecurity and technology risk, not a one-time project.

  1. 01

    Govern

    Define responsibilities, expectations, and decision-making authority for cybersecurity, AI, and technology risk.

  2. 02

    Identify

    Understand your systems, information, vendors, technology use, and emerging sources of risk.

  3. 03

    Assess

    Evaluate cybersecurity, operational, data, vendor, and compliance risk.

  4. 04

    Prioritize

    Determine which risks need attention now, which can wait, and where resources will have the greatest impact.

  5. 05

    Control

    Develop and implement the policies, processes, safeguards, and accountability needed to manage those risks.

  6. 06

    Monitor

    Keep the program current as risk, controls, and requirements change.
