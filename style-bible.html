Section 03

Logo Usage

Always use the official logo file at /Images/logo-new.png. Do not stretch, recolor, add drop shadows, or place on busy backgrounds.

On White ✓
On Light Gray ✓
On Primary Blue ✓

Minimum Size

60px height in mobile nav. 100px height in desktop nav. Never smaller than 48px.

Clear Space

Maintain at least half the logo height as clear space on all four sides.

File Format

PNG for web. Request SVG from brand owner for print or large-format use.

Don't Do

No rotation, no color filters, no cropping, no unofficial variations.

Section 09

Voice & Tone

BCS speaks plainly, confidently, and practically — like a trusted advisor, not a vendor. We translate technical complexity into business outcomes without talking down to the reader.

DO — Brand Voice

  • Speak plainly — say "risk" not "threat surface"
  • Focus on the client's outcome, not our process
  • Use "we" and "your" — make it a conversation
  • Be direct: strong verbs, short sentences
  • Show expertise through clarity, not jargon
  • Be confident without overselling
  • Reference "Bluegrass" as a nod to community & roots

DON'T — Off-Brand

  • Don't use fear tactics to sell services
  • Don't stack acronyms (NIST, SOC2, HIPAA) without context
  • Avoid passive voice and corporate filler
  • Don't promise outcomes you can't guarantee
  • Never condescend — assume the reader is smart
  • Don't make every headline a question
  • Avoid exclamation points in professional copy

Tagline

"Bridging the gap between findings and fixes."

Use verbatim in hero sections and email signatures. Do not paraphrase. Rendered in Gold (#d4a017) italic at tagline scale.

Headings

Title Case for section headings. Sentence case for body sub-headings and card titles.

CTAs

Action verbs: "Get Started", "Contact Us", "View Services". Not "Click Here" or "Submit".

Blog Posts

Conversational but expert. 800–1,500 words. Practical takeaways in every post.

Company Name

"Bluegrass Cybersecurity Solutions" on first mention. "BCS" acceptable thereafter in informal contexts.

Section 10

Accessibility

BCS targets WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. Every design decision must account for keyboard navigation, color contrast, and screen readers.

Skip Link

A visually hidden "Skip to content" link appears at top-of-page for keyboard users. It becomes visible on focus. Class: .skip-link

Focus Rings

Interactive elements show a visible focus indicator. The blue focus ring on form inputs is box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(74,144,199,0.18).

Aria Labels

Hamburger button uses aria-label="Toggle navigation" and aria-expanded. All images have meaningful alt attributes.

Semantic HTML

Use <header>, <nav>, <main>, <section>, <footer>. Heading hierarchy must not skip levels.

Color Contrast

All text must meet 4.5:1 minimum (AA). White on Primary Blue passes at 9.8:1. White on Light Green passes at 4.6:1.

Motion

All transitions are 0.2–0.3s. Respect prefers-reduced-motion by removing transforms and fading instead of sliding.

Images

Decorative images: alt="". Informational images: descriptive alt text. Logo: alt="Bluegrass Cybersecurity Solutions".

Loading

Below-the-fold images use loading="lazy". Critical above-fold images (logo, hero) load eagerly.

Key Contrast Ratios

9.8:1 White on Primary Blue — PASS AAA
4.6:1 White on Light Green — PASS AA
6.5:1 White on Accent Blue — PASS AA
12.6:1 Body Gray on Light Gray — PASS AAA
2.9:1 White on Gold — Large text only

Gold (#d4a017) should only be used for large display text (≥18px bold or ≥24px regular) or decorative elements — never for small body copy.
