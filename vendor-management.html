Vendor Management

Bring structure to third-party oversight, critical vendor risk, and examiner-ready documentation.

Talk about vendor management All services

Make vendor files useful for decisions and defensible for review.

For third-party risk, we help identify critical vendors, tier risk, review contracts and SOC reports, track due diligence, and document ongoing monitoring. The result is a vendor program that shows how risk is evaluated, accepted, monitored, and reported.

What this helps with

  • Critical vendor inventory cleanup
  • Due diligence and SOC report review
  • Contract and SLA risk questions
  • Board and management vendor reporting
