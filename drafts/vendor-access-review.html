Vendor Access Reviews: What to Check Before Renewal

Published June 23, 2026

Why Renewal Is a Good Trigger

Vendor reviews often focus on contracts, insurance, SOC reports, and financial condition. Those matter, but access deserves the same attention. Contract renewal is a natural point to confirm who can reach your systems, data, portals, and support tools.

What to Review

  • Named accounts: confirm active vendor users still need access.
  • Privileged access: verify administrative roles, remote support permissions, and emergency accounts.
  • Shared credentials: identify and replace shared accounts wherever possible.
  • Authentication: confirm MFA and password requirements match your expectations.
  • Offboarding: test how quickly access is removed when vendor staff change roles or leave.

Document the Decision

The review should end with a short record: what was checked, what changed, who approved continued access, and which exceptions remain open. That record becomes useful evidence during audits, exams, and incident reviews.
