Filing a SOC report isn't reviewing it. Work through these fields so the review is written down and defensible.

Report basics

A SOC 2 Type II covering the right period and trust criteria is usually what you want for a security-relevant service. A bridge letter covers the gap between the report's end date and today — ask for one if there's a gap.

What to read

  • Auditor's opinion is unqualified (clean). Any qualification is noted and understood.
  • Scope covers the service you actually use and the right trust criteria (security at minimum).
  • Period is current; bridge letter obtained if there's a gap to today.
  • Exceptions / deviations in the testing results read and assessed for impact on you.
  • Subservice organizations identified (carve-out vs inclusive) and material ones noted for their own review.
  • Complementary User Entity Controls (CUECs) listed — the controls you are responsible for — and mapped below.
