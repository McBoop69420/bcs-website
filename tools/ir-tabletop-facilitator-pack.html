Facilitator pack

60-Minute IR Tabletop Facilitator Pack

Run a credible incident response exercise in an hour with almost no prep. Validate roles, escalation, communications, and evidence capture.

Version 1.0 · Last updated November 5, 2026

Before you start (5 minutes of prep)

  • Invite the people who'd actually be in the room: a decision-maker, IT/security, and someone from communications or operations.
  • Pick one scenario below. Don't tell participants the injects in advance.
  • Name a facilitator (keeps time, reads injects) and a scribe (fills the debrief sheet).
  • Set the ground rule: play your real role with the tools and authority you actually have today.

Agenda (60 minutes)

TimeSegmentPurpose
0–5Set the sceneRead the scenario; confirm who's in the room and their role.
5–20Inject 1 — DetectionHow is this found, triaged, and escalated? Who gets called?
20–35Inject 2 — EscalationIt's worse than it looked. Containment decisions, insurer/vendor calls.
35–50Inject 3 — CommunicationsA customer and a regulator ask questions. Who speaks, and what's said?
50–60DebriefCapture what worked, what broke, and the follow-up actions.

Scenario A — Ransomware

  • Inject 1: Staff report files renamed with a strange extension; a shared drive is inaccessible.
  • Inject 2: A ransom note appears; backups for one critical system can't be found.
  • Inject 3: A local reporter emails asking to confirm an "outage"; a customer calls about downtime.

Scenario B — Business email compromise

  • Inject 1: Finance notices a login alert for an executive's mailbox from an unusual location.
  • Inject 2: A fraudulent wire request was sent from that mailbox yesterday; funds may have moved.
  • Inject 3: A vendor asks why they received odd payment-change instructions from your domain.

Questions to drive each inject

  • Who makes the call to declare an incident, and how is it recorded?
  • What's the first containment action, and who's authorized to take it?
  • Which external parties get called, in what order, and by when? (insurer, IR firm, regulator, counsel)
  • Where is evidence captured, and who owns the timeline?
  • Who speaks for the organization, and what's the holding statement?
