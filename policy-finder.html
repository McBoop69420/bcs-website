Information Security Policy

Sets the ground rules for access control, data handling, and accountability — the document examiners ask for first.

See a free example

3.4 Access Control — Access to systems containing nonpublic customer information is granted on a least-privilege, role-based basis and reviewed at least quarterly. All privileged and administrative accounts require multi-factor authentication. Access is revoked within one business day of termination or role change.

Documents recovery objectives and testing so an outage doesn't turn into a repeat exam finding.

5.1 Recovery Time Objectives — Critical systems are assigned a maximum tolerable downtime and a recovery time objective (RTO), reviewed annually and validated through a documented BCP test. Deviations identified during testing are logged and tracked to remediation.
