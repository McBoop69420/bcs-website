Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Who we are

Bluegrass Cybersecurity Solutions is a trade name of Bluegrass Compliance Solutions LLC. Questions about this policy go to info@bluegrasscybersecurity.com.

What we collect

  • What you enter in a form: your name, email address, and organization, your consent, and what you tell us, such as the service you’re interested in, your timeline, your message, the template you asked for, or the industry you chose and the policies recommended to you in the Policy Finder.
  • Details recorded with a submission: the date and time, which form you used, and the country your connection came from. We work out the country from your IP address and don’t store the address with your submission.
  • Your IP address, briefly: we hold it for about 10 minutes to limit repeated submissions, then it is deleted automatically.
  • Email: whatever you choose to send us.

We don’t take payment details on this website. Purchases are arranged by email, and we’ll tell you how payment works, and who processes it, before you pay.

Policy Finder answers stay in your browser. We only receive what you submit in the form at the end of it.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, including sending a template or tool you asked for and following up on it
  • To tell you when a tool you downloaded is updated
  • To protect the site from spam and abuse
  • To understand overall site traffic, using totals rather than information about individuals

We don’t sell your information or add you to marketing lists.

Who handles it for us

We use a small number of providers to run the site and read your messages:

  • Cloudflare delivers and protects the site, runs the Turnstile human check, stores form submissions, forwards our email, and provides visitor totals. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts the site’s files. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Google hosts the mailbox that receives our email. See Google’s privacy policy.

We also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone’s safety.

Cookies and analytics

Our own code doesn’t set cookies. Cloudflare Turnstile checks your browser to tell people from automated traffic, and Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without using cookies.

How long we keep it

Form submissions are deleted automatically 24 months after you send them. At least once a year, we also delete emails older than 24 months from people who didn’t become clients. You can ask us to delete your information sooner. We keep client records for as long as our agreement or the law requires.

Your choices

You can withdraw your consent, or ask us to show, correct, or delete what we hold about you, by emailing info@bluegrasscybersecurity.com. We’ll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under privacy law, and we’ll honor those too.

How we protect it

The site only works over encrypted connections, form submissions can’t be downloaded without a secret key, and access is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. To report a security problem, see our security contact.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn’t directed at children. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Where it’s processed

We operate in the United States. Our providers may process information in other countries.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
